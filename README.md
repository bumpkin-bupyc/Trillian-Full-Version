<a id="overview"></a>

Trillian is a **multiplatform chat client** designed to unify your communication needs by connecting to multiple instant messaging services like MSN, Yahoo! Messenger, ICQ, and AIM. With the **latest version**, you can easily access all your chat conversations in one place, making it the perfect solution for users with multiple accounts.

## ⚡ Key Features
<a id="key-features"></a>

### Core Functionality:
- **Multi-IM Compatibility** — Connect simultaneously to Windows Live Messenger, Yahoo! Messenger, ICQ, and AIM without logging in to each service individually.
- **Text, Audio, and Video Calls** — Communicate in the way that suits you best; whether it's text, voice, or video.
- **Customizable Interface** — Utilize skins and customization options to organize your contacts and enhance your chatting experience.
- **Cross-Device Synchronization** — Start conversations on your desktop and continue on your mobile device without losing history.
- **Social Network Integration** — Update your profiles on Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn, and Foursquare simultaneously.

### Advanced Features:
- **Emoticons and Plug-in Support** — Use a variety of emoticons in conversations and enhance functionality with additional plug-ins.
- **Dedicated Contact Icons** — Easily identify your contacts by their source through unique icons displayed on the interface.
- **Ongoing Updates** — Regular improvements ensure you benefit from the latest features and enhancements.
